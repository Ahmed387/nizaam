# Project rules

- Stock movements use document dates, not `created_at`, for correct point-in-time balances.
- Set `public.stock_mv_ctx(...)` immediately before transfer quantity changes.
- Validate backdated transfers with `stock_balance_as_of`; block insufficient stock without override.
- Voucher journals follow `voucher_date`; treasury periods use synchronized `treasury.transaction_date`, never `created_at`.
- Date filters use Cairo timestamp bounds or direct SQL-date comparison.
- Write recipient/notes only via `set_document_annotations`; leave financial fields untouched.
- Compute contact running balances before filters; dated views add a prior-balance row.
- Reads over 1,000 rows use `fetchAllRows`, `.order("id")`, and lean columns.
- Save sales only via atomic `process_sale_full`; require shifts and restrict backdating to admins.
- Cancel vouchers through `cancel_voucher` reversal rows; never delete treasury history.
- Cap return refunds at actual cash/transfer paid; the remainder is credit.
- Default today values use `cairoTodayDay()`, never UTC slicing.
- Contact deposits («إيداع تحت الحساب») go to the till of the branch chosen in the dialog (`create_contact_credit_deposit(..., p_branch_id)`, 0206); `PARTNER-CASH` is partner-capital-only; `MAIN-TREASURY` holds no customer / supplier money.
- Admin reports use `contact_branch_balances`, `get_branch_wallet_balances`, and `v_contact_balances.ledger_balance` for their respective scopes.
- Show negative contact credit separately from treasury cash and flows.
- Branch queries use `scopeToBranch` with the route branch id; cross-branch views are admin/HQ only.
- Filter payments in-DB via `invoice_payments!inner`, not a client id list.

- Prepaid customer credit is only `contacts.credit_balance` (negative = available) consumed via `process_sale_full.p_credit_to_apply`; `settlement` is retired and `treasury` rejects non-cash methods by trigger — why: one flow, no phantom cash.
- `treasury` has no client INSERT grant; cash movements are written only by SECURITY DEFINER RPCs (e.g. `disburse_employee_advance`) — why: no phantom or half-written cash from the browser.
- `contacts.credit_balance` is a derived cache of Σ `customer_credit_log.balance_delta`, synced by trigger; direct updates are blocked — why: one source of truth for prepaid credit.
- Purchase invoices save via `record_shipment_with_credit` (shipment + plan header + supplier advance deduction atomically); supplier balances on shipments are computed from the contact ledger, never from stored `previous_balance/current_balance` — why: no half-saved invoices or stale balances.
- Guarded RPCs keep their whole body in one function (no `__impl` split copies); tenant/role guards run at the top of that body — why: one callable path, no unguarded twin.
- Never `GRANT EXECUTE … TO authenticated` in a loop over all SECURITY DEFINER functions. Internal helpers stay owner/service_role only; a new browser RPC is granted by name and added to the allow-list in `security_exposed_definer_functions()` in the same migration — why: definer functions bypass RLS (SEC-001, migration 0049).
- The browser never calls `process_sale`; online and offline sales both go through `process_sale_full` — why: shift gate and admin-only backdating live there.
- A new browser RPC is exposed by inserting its name into `security_rpc_allowlist` in the same migration (0050 replaced the hard-coded list) — why: the migration-end check fails on anything else.
- `profiles.tenant_id` never changes except by the super admin or service_role (`guard_profile_tenant`); new auth users get their company from `app_metadata.tenant_id`, never `user_metadata` — why: `current_tenant_id()` reads it (SEC-002).
- Every role helper (`has_role`, `is_admin`, `is_owner`, `has_central_access`, `user_in_branch`, `user_can_write_branch`) counts a role only inside the user's own company; only `super_admin` is platform-wide — why: no cross-company RPC calls.
- Companies and their owners are created only from Super Admin → Tenants (`bootstrap-tenant`, super admin only); there is no public sign-up. A company with documents is never deleted — suspend it — why: owner rule, history is kept.
- The Super Admin profit card reads `super_tenants_monthly_profit` (GL); its figures stay hidden until `platform_flags.super_profit_card` is switched on after Phase 5 — why: no estimated numbers.
- Products with stock/document history cannot be deleted (RESTRICT FKs + guard trigger; only the guarded branch reset purges) — why: protect historical reports and COGS.
- Payments of backdated documents pass the document date to `record_payment_transaction(p_payment_date)` so treasury/payment rows inherit it — why: periods follow accounting dates.
- `vouchers.treasury_id` is NOT NULL with FK to `treasury` (ON DELETE CASCADE) — why: no voucher can exist without its cash movement; admin purges of treasury remove the voucher with it.
- Partners get central read-only access to all branches (client treats `partner` like `viewer`; a trigger auto-adds the `viewer` role) — why: one central read path, no writes outside their portal.
- Historical import tables are scoped by `branch_id` (auto-filled from `branch_name` by trigger); `branch_name` is display-only — why: no string-based access checks.
- No hard delete of documents or money rows (invoices, shipments, returns, purchase returns, vouchers, payments, credit log, partner transactions, audit logs): correct by Cancel only. `zz_block_hard_delete` rejects direct deletes and lets only FK cascades through — why: owner rule, history is kept (SEC-003, migration 0051).
- Purchase invoices are cancelled only via `cancel_purchase_invoice` (stock out in the stock ledger, batches closed, plan cancelled, journal reversed, audit row); a cancelled invoice is frozen. Every money/total reader of `shipments` filters `status = 'active'` — why: cancelled purchases are outside statements, cards and reports.
- Every cancel/edit RPC starts with `assert_access(branch_id, permission)` (company, suspension, branch, permission). Server defaults in `role_permission_default` mirror `DEFAULT_ROLE_PERMISSIONS` in `src/lib/roles.ts` — change both together — why: the browser check alone is not a guard.
- `audit_log` and `price_audit_log` are append-only (no client update/delete); the app ships no seed or data-reset screens — why: the audit trail must be trustworthy.
- The browser has no INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE on money and document tables (invoices and items, payments, plans and installments, shipments and items, batches, returns, purchase returns, vouchers, treasury, journal, stock movements, credit log, contacts, partner transactions). Writes go through guarded RPCs: `save_contact` / `delete_contact`, `finalize_sale_pricing`, `create_payment_plan`, `update_shipment_date`, `create_purchase_return`; a new write path is a new RPC, never a new grant — why: amounts and quantities are computed and checked on the server (SEC-004, migration 0052).
- A purchase invoice's journal entry always sits on its `invoice_date`: pass a backdated date inside `record_shipment_with_credit.p_header.invoice_date`, and change it later only through `update_shipment_date` — why: backdated documents belong to their own period.
- Audit rows are written only by the server: the browser calls `logAudit()` → `log_audit_event`, which takes the user, name and company from the session; `audit_log` has no client INSERT — why: no spoofed names or NULL users (Step 5, migration 0053).
- Product fields change only through `update_product_details`, prices through `update_product_price_with_reason` (cost admin-only, reason logged), bulk/delete/low-stock through admin RPCs; manual counts through `create_stock_adjustment` (system quantity read at save) — why: stock value and COGS are not editable from the browser.
- `doc_ledger_allocation`, `contact_branch_balances`, `v_contact_balances`, `invoice_outstanding_debt` and `v_supplier_ledger` are retired from the browser (netted or partial formulas); a contact's customer side and supplier side are never netted anywhere — why: owner rule.
- Sales are saved only through `process_sale_once(p_client_request_id, …)`; the id is made when the sale is created and kept in the offline queue — why: a replay never creates a second invoice (H-01). Transfer fees are computed on the server from `transfer_fees` (H-08).
- The owner's personal ledger is cancel-only (`save_personal_entry` / `cancel_personal_entry`); cancelled lines stay listed and leave every total — why: no hard delete.
- `money_movements` / `payment_allocations` (Step 6A, migration 0054) are the single subledger: one movement per money/credit event on ONE side (customer or supplier, never netted); allocations pair a settle movement with a debt of the same contact and side. Rows are written only by server functions, never updated except cancel/supersede, never deleted — why: one source of truth with full history.
- Accounts 2150 Customer Advances, 1460 Supplier Advances and 1910 Inter-Branch Current Account exist for every company (seeded for new ones); nothing posts to them until 6D — why: owner's chart of accounts.
- The ledger is filled only by `ledger_sync_contact` / `ledger_sync_walkin` (deferred `zz_ledger_capture` triggers on the source tables, once per contact per transaction) and `ledger_backfill`. Capture rules live in `ledger_desired_movements`: one origin per economic event; advance use ('credit_balance' / 'credit_applied'), old netting ('settlement') and voided plan mirrors are not money. Scope is 2026-01-01 onwards; the historical archive and the owner's personal ledger are never read — why: owner rule (Step 6B, migration 0055).
- `ledger_allocate` is the only allocation code: direct to the movement's document first, then company-wide FIFO by document date; customer and supplier sides never meet; allocations in closed periods are frozen. Until 6C the ledger is shadow-only: errors go to `ledger_sync_errors` and never block a business action; check `ledger_reconciliation()` (unexplained must stay 0).
- Every balance on screen comes from the unified ledger readers in `src/lib/ledger.ts` (`contacts_page`, `contact_side_balances`, `ledger_contact_overview` / `_documents` / `_money` / `_statement`, `ledger_document_status`, `ledger_position`, `ledger_aging`). A side balance is + owed to us (customer) / + we owe (supplier); − is the advance (2150 / 1460). A branch view = that branch's documents and money, cross-branch uses shown as their own lines; branch staff see other branches only as totals — why: one engine, global totals, branch-isolated transactions (Step 6C, migration 0056).
- The contact file is `ContactFile` (360°): customer account and supplier account in separate tabs, scope all branches (admins) or one branch, documents open / closed / cancelled / before 2026, money, returns, plans — why: owner requirement.
- Contact opening balances sit on the contact's branch (`contacts.opening_branch_id` = its «سهل» branch, else `contacts.branch_id`; `ledger_opening_branch`, 0206), one opening journal entry per branch (`post_contacts_opening_entries`, source key = contacts section + branch); opening cash is also a till row (category «رصيد افتتاحي», no voucher). `post_opening_balances` never deletes: a re-posted section is reversed (journal reversal, till reversal, opening batches emptied as `opening_reversed`), stock is added not overwritten, and a sold-from stock opening cannot be re-posted — why: cancel-only, tills include opening cash.
- A contact is archived, never deleted (`delete_contact` and approved delete requests archive; `zz_block_hard_delete` on contacts); archiving is refused while either side has a balance — why: no hard delete.
- `system_integrity_audit()` (SystemHealth, admin dashboard) is the read-only consistency check: opening vs on-account money across branches, duplicates, advances next to open debt, documents vs lines, returns vs quantities, vouchers vs till, till vs cash accounts, stock vs batches, cancelled documents still in the journal. It never fixes anything — why: problems are visible, corrections stay cancel-only.
- Write paths decide on the ledger, never on `invoice_outstanding_debt` or `contacts.credit_balance`: `settle_contact_invoices_sovereign` (open per invoice = `ledger_doc_open`), `process_sale_full` (advance = `ledger_side_advance(customer)`), `allocate_sale_return_settlement` (open and money paid from the ledger), `apply_customer_credit` (writes nothing — FIFO applies advances), `apply_supplier_credit` (supplier side only), `cancel_invoice_with_refund` (paid counted once). Each refreshes the contact's ledger first (`ledger_refresh_contact`) — why: one engine, no netting (Step 6D, migration 0057).
- The GL for customer / supplier money comes from the ledger (`ledger_gl_sync`, at commit with the capture): movement entries in the branch where the money moved (cash ↔ 2150 / 1460 pool; return credit and refunds through 1200 / 2100), allocation entries on the effective date (pool → 1200 / 2100), and through 1910 when the money and the document are in different branches. The old triggers (`invoice_payment`, `supplier_payment`, contact vouchers) no longer post from 2026-01-01; their old entries were reversed on their own dates. Nothing in a closed period is touched (`ledger_gl_skipped`). Check `ledger_gl_reconciliation()`: every branch till = its 11xx, 1910 total = 0, 2150 / 1460 = ledger advances — why: tills match the books.
- `contacts.credit_balance` is only a mirror of the ledger advance (old readers); its old values are kept in `ledger_credit_balance_snapshot` — why: one source of truth.
- Document status of a past period is "as of" its last day (`p_as_of` on `ledger_document_status` / `ledger_contact_documents` / `ledger_aging`, replayed by `ledger_simulate_as_of`) — why: historical reports must not change with later payments.
- `void_payment_transaction` voids any payment transaction (plan or not): till and fee reversed, reversal row, mirrored invoice payment voided, plans recomputed from what still stands (`plan_recompute`) — why: cancel-only, installments always add up.
- "اسألني" never runs model-written SQL: the model picks a report from `ai_report_catalog` and `ai_report` runs it (invoker rights, read-only, company / branch / permission scoped). `ai_sql` is revoked — why: SEC-005.
- A SECURITY DEFINER function that takes a branch / product / shift / company id must check access to it; unguarded ones were wrapped (`*__unguarded` + guard) — why: SEC-007.
- Voucher cards classify by `vouchers.flow_class` (customer / supplier / customer_refund / internal / operating / other_income), set on insert from party, category, till row and the `app.contact_money` flag — not by the typed party alone — why: till vouchers of collections were counted as "other income" (Step 6E, migration 0058).
- Payment-method cards of the sale / purchase screens read `ledger_money_flow` NET (money in minus money given back, incl. cash back on cancelled archive invoices); the part of a period before 2026-01-01 comes from its documents; a ledger error keeps the documents' figures — why: plan payments and contact-file payments were missing.
- Archive invoice = dated before 2026-01-01 AND recorded before it (or `is_migrated` / `migration_batch_id`): `ledger_invoice_is_archive`. Cancelling one from 2026 is a prior-period sales return: rule J = (part of what the invoice still asked for on 1 Jan 2026 that his opening balance carries — the opening balance is shared by his archive cancellations in cancel order, never more than it) − (what he paid on it from 2026, already ledger money) + (all paid, when refunded on account); J > 0 credits him, J < 0 charges back (kind refund); ONE GL entry on the cancel day (Dr 4900 net + 2200 VAT / Cr 2150, 1200, 1100 cash back, 4200 kept) — `ledger_archive_cancel_parts` / `_lines`; its revenue entry is not reversed and its COGS is reversed on the cancel day, never deleted. Rule E (cash back as a ledger refund) is for 2026 invoices only.
- Cancelling an archive PURCHASE invoice: `cancel_purchase_invoice` reverses last year's entry as before; rule K (`ledger_archive_shipment_parts`) settles the supplier side with (part of what it still asked for on 1 Jan 2026 that his opening carries, shared in cancel order) + (paid on its plan before 2026, which stays with him as our advance); books = the usual Dr 1460 / Cr 2100.
- Contacts' opening balances in the books: the books on 2026-01-01 = entries dated before it + opening entries (`ledger_opening_books`), matched to the ledger opening by `match_opening_books` (preview, then ONE entry for the difference; the previous match entry is reversed) — never post the full opening on top of 2025 books (it counts twice).
- New invoices / shipments dated before 2026-01-01 are refused (`a1_guard_pre_ledger_date`), except migrated rows or `app.allow_pre_ledger_date = '1'`; older ones are flagged L7 — why: they are in no balance (not in the opening, not in the ledger).
- Transfer fees: every path writes ONE till row per fee (expense row with `app.skip_expense_till`, till row `is_fee = true`); old doubles are paired one-to-one by `duplicate_fee_till_rows` and corrected cancel-only by `repair_duplicate_fee_till_rows(true)` (claims the voucher first, skips closed periods).
- `cancel_voucher`'s guard uses the alias `gv` (the alias `v` clashed with its row variable and made every cancel fail).
- Ledger work tables are indexed and journal numbers come from an index (big contacts / months stay fast); keep it that way when changing `ledger_allocate` or `post_journal`.
- Cost of goods is the branch MOVING AVERAGE, not FIFO (owner decision, migration 0071): purchases / opening stock / transfers in (at the sender's average) / sale returns (at the cost they left with) move the average; sales, purchase returns, transfers out and counts never change it. `product_cost_ledger(product)` replays a product row from its documents; `product_avg_cost(product, as_of)` is the cost at a moment (sold ahead of stock → the next incoming cost); `invoice_items` get it on insert (`a1_invoice_item_avg_cost`) and `products.avg_cost` is only a cache. Batches still track quantities. `recompute_average_costs(from, to)` re-costs sales (service role) — why: one cost per product per branch, sales never read purchase documents.
- Owner money/stock reports: `owner_business_snapshot` (net sales | collected in hand | credit still open, profit at average cost) and `branch_stock_report(from, to, branch?)` (opening, purchased, returns, transfers, sold at cost, count differences, left × average) — migration 0072.
- Payroll (migration 0074): the absence deduction is computed only on the server (`employee_salary_preview`, used by `pay_employee_salary`): leave is a paid status, only recorded absent / half-day / hourly days reduce pay, a working day with nothing recorded is a normal day. The pay screen sends bonus and extra deduction separately; a payment is undone only by `cancel_salary_payment` (voucher reversal, advance instalments returned, journal reversed, row kept as `cancelled`); one active salary per employee per month. Salary transfer fees come from `calculate_payment_fee` — why: owner rule, cancel-only.
- RLS policies test branches with `branch_id = ANY ((SELECT my_branch_ids())::uuid[])` / `my_write_branch_ids()` and wrap `auth.uid()`, `current_tenant_id()` and role helpers in `( SELECT … )` (migration 0075). Never call `user_in_branch(auth.uid(), branch_id)` per row in a new policy — why: speed (the till went from 1.4 s to 10 ms, the audit log from 5 s to 20 ms).
- A screen calling an RPC shows `rpcErrorText()` (src/lib/rpcError.ts) so a database without the newest migrations gets a clear message instead of "Could not find the function … in the schema cache".
- Stock quantities that no document explains stay set to the physical count with NO adjustment document and NO journal entry (owner decision, 2026-10-11). `branch_stock_report.unrecorded_*` only shows the difference for information — never create «تسوية» documents for it.
- The platform users page reads `super_list_users()` (Auth accounts + company + roles, super admin only); profiles and user_roles have no FK between them, so never embed `user_roles(...)` inside a `profiles` select. Passwords are reset by the platform owner through the `super-set-password` edge function (migration 0076).
- The admin home reads `system_integrity_summary` from `system_integrity_cache` (refreshed hourly by cron «integrity-cache»); since migration 0116 it returns the last cached result whatever its age (`checked_at` shows it) and runs the live audit only when a company has none — why: half of every hour each admin home ran the 10–35 s audit live and hit the 8 s limit (17 × 500, 2026-10-25). The SystemHealth page still runs `system_integrity_audit` live.
- Treasury tables list reversal rows (`REVERSAL_CATEGORIES` in src/lib/treasuryReversals.ts: voucher cancel, cancelled returns, money back on a cancelled invoice, payment voids / edits and their fee, reversing corrections) in their own tab; balances and period cards keep every row.
- Voucher `flow_class`: on a payment, a named customer party wins over the category (`customer_refund`) — migration 0078.
- Realtime refresh (`useRealtimeRefresh`) gathers changes for 1.5 s, reloads a page at most once per 5 s, and never while its tab is hidden (reloads once when shown). The presence heartbeat runs every 3 min only while the tab is visible. The branch dashboard reads its invoices ONCE (earliest needed day → today) and cuts every card from that read — why: pages took 20–30 s on the Micro database from duplicated reads and reload storms.
- `fetchAllRows` reads page 1 alone, then the next pages four at a time (each round trip from Egypt ≈ 0.5 s). `hasRole` from `useAuth` is memoized on the roles — keep effect dependencies on stable values (booleans), never on functions recreated per render. A token refresh for the same user does not reload the auth context within 10 min (realtime reloads it on real role / branch changes).
- Sale / purchase screens: cards «الإجمالي» | «نقدي» (nothing left on the document) | «آجل» (still open), each with its count, total and how it was paid (cash / InstaPay / wallet / bank from `ledger_document_paid_by_method`, return credit shown apart), the credit card also shows what is still open (migration 0079). Return screens have no «خصم من الرصيد» box under the top cards. Stock transfers are valued at the sender's moving average at the transfer moment (`stock_transfer_values`, migration 0080): total card (accepted / rejected / pending) and a value column.
- Bonus / penalty («بونص» / «جزاء») during the month live in `employee_adjustments` (migration 0081), written only by `add_employee_adjustment` / `cancel_employee_adjustment` (cancel-only; refused once a paid salary used them). Nothing moves in the till when they are recorded: `employee_salary_preview` returns them under `adjustments`, and `pay_employee_salary` adds them to the salary's bonuses / deductions and links them (`salary_id`); `cancel_salary_payment` frees them — why: they reach the voucher, till, salary expense and reports once, through the salary.
- Attendance can be marked in bulk (several employees × several days) on `AttendanceBoard`: existing rows are updated by id, new rows inserted; future days and employees the user can't edit are skipped.
- An employee's name anywhere in the app is `EmployeeLink` (src/components/employees/EmployeeLink.tsx) → `/admin/employees/:id` for users who can open that page; plain text for others.
- The sales table's payment-method columns come from the ledger allocations (`ledger_document_paid_by_method`), so a credit invoice paid later by voucher / contact file shows the method and amount.
- Money «تحت الحساب» (an advance: money left before the documents, a surplus, a return from a paid / other document, an opening credit) is used on a document ONLY by choice (owner rule, migration 0082): the cashier's mandatory «تخصم من تحت حسابه؟» on the sale (`process_sale_full.p_credit_to_apply` → invoice_payments `credit_balance`), the purchase screen (`record_shipment_with_credit` `apply_credit` + `credit_amount`; default is now NOT to apply), or «سدد من تحت حسابه» in the contact file (`settle_from_account` → invoice_payments `credit_applied` / payment_transactions `credit_balance`). `ledger_allocate` order: direct (money / return on its own document) → explicit uses (mode `explicit`) → restricted FIFO (receipts / payments / deposits settle only documents that already existed on their day; refunds / charges are settled from anything on account). A return from a document still open reduces that document at once. Applied to the past too (2026-10-13). «له تحت حسابه وعليه فواتير مفتوحة» is a normal state (integrity L1 = info) — why: owner rule, no automatic use of a customer's / supplier's money.
- «التصنيف» of a sale / purchase invoice: نقدي = all paid in money with the document itself; آجل = anything else (paid later, from account, from a return, or still open). `invoice_settlement` / `shipment_settlement` (migration 0083) hold, per document, class / state / at_sale / later / from_account / from_return / cash / instapay / wallet / bank / paid / remaining; refreshed by `ledger_allocate` (all documents of the contact) and a deferred trigger on the document; the browser reads them embedded (`settle:invoice_settlement(...)`, `!inner` when filtering) — the sale / purchase filters «التصنيف» / «حالة السداد» / «طريقة الدفع» run in the database on them. Paid / remaining shown in the table still come from `ledger_document_status` (as of the period end). Labels: «من تحت حسابه», «من مرتجع», «لم يتم الدفع».
- A cancellation / correction takes effect on the ORIGINAL day (owner rule, migration 0084): `till_cancel_on_original_date(treasury_id, reason)` is the one cancel path — it cancels the till row's voucher (kept in «الملغاة» with its reason), writes the «إلغاء سند» till reversal on the original day and reverses the voucher's journal on the original day (`app.reverse_on_original_date` → `reverse_journal_for_source`); a closed period moves it to the first open day (`ledger_effective_date`). `cancel_voucher`, `void_invoice_payment`, `_void_payment_transaction_exec` use it and make NO new receipt / payment voucher; `edit_invoice_payment` / `edit_payment_transaction` cancel the original voucher and record the corrected amount on the original day. Money really given back later is a refund voucher / return on its own day, not a cancellation. Integrity checks C1 (reversal not on the original day) and C2 (a void / edit that made a new voucher) must stay 0 — why: every period shows what really happened (2026-10-13; IN03646, 5 payment edits and every old «إلغاء سند» were moved to their original day).
- Cancelling a sale / purchase invoice asks ONE question about the money paid ON it (migration 0085): «تحت حسابه» (credit — stays as the advance), «يسترد فلوسه» / «المورد رجّع فلوسنا» (refund on the cancel day, method chosen; sale `refund_pay_method`, purchase `money_action = 'refund'` + a `purchase_cancel_refund` till receipt the ledger charges to his side), «غلط ومفيش فلوس» (void — the document's own payments are cancelled on their own day through the void paths, no new voucher). Money that reached it from the account (advance use, return credit, a general receipt FIFO applied) goes back to the account by itself and is never refunded by the cancel. Option 3 is refused for a payment that is part of one hand-over that also paid other documents (`SETTLE-` reference), in a closed period, or on an archive purchase invoice. Preview: `document_cancel_preview` — why: owner rule, till / ledger / document never disagree.
- `cancel_voucher` on a voucher that carries a payment cancels THAT payment (`voucher_payment_link`: plan / contact payment → `_void_payment_transaction_exec`, invoice payment → `void_invoice_payment` on that exact till row); a fee voucher alone cancels only the fee; a refund voucher of a cancelled document = the refund did not happen (money back on account); a receipt whose money was given back is refused until its refund voucher is cancelled — why: IN03457 / IN04863 (2026-10-13) left the till and the ledger apart.
- Paying from the contact file always settles the oldest invoice first (no selection); the supplier spread follows the purchase invoice date. Paying a specific invoice from the invoice itself stays allowed — why: owner rule (0085).
- `edit_invoice` never touches the till (`sync_invoice_cash_treasury` is retired: it rewrote amounts and removed till rows); a payment changes only through edit / void of that payment — why: IN03486.
- Changing a sale invoice's date (`update_invoice_date_sovereign`) moves the money paid WITH it — the payments of the invoice's own day, their till rows, receipt vouchers and plan mirrors (`invoice_move_sale_payments`, migration 0086); payments of later days stay on their day — why: the «نقدي» card (invoice date) and the receipt vouchers (voucher date) must fall in the same period (IN04853: invoice 10/3, receipt 6/10).
